How to Play

💧 Goal: Rotate the pipes until every piece connects back to the central source hub and the liquid flows through all conduits.

🔄 Rotate: Tap any tile to rotate it 90° clockwise. Right-click or long-press rotates counter-clockwise.

✨ Magic Hint: Tap the wand button at the bottom to solve and lock a tricky tile.

🌐 Wrap-Around Mode: When enabled, pipes exiting the screen on one edge flow seamlessly into the opposite border.
