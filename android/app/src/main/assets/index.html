How to Play

💧 Goal: Rotate the conduits until every pipe connects back to the central source hub and liquid flows everywhere.

🔄 Rotate: Tap any tile to rotate 90° clockwise. Right-click or long-press rotates counter-clockwise.

💡 Lightbulb Hint: Tap the wand/lightbulb button to solve and lock a tricky conduit in place.

🌀 Color-Coded Portals: When portals are active, water entering Portal A teleports and flows out of its matching Portal A on another part of the board!
